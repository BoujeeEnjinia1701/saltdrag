# SaltDrag user requirements

What the people who would make, use, fund and permit SaltDrag need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in SDG-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix SDG-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). The sources are the user and stakeholder tables of SDG-PRB-001 (Tables 1 and 2). None of these needs has yet been checked with an Agariya family or a producer organisation; the co-design partner reviews them before any trial. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Usability | Stay out of the pan | We gather the salt without standing in the brine or walking on the pan at any stage of the work | High | Agariya salt-making families; women salt workers (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Usability | Effort we can keep up | Women in the family can work the tool through a working session without great upper-body strength | High | Women salt workers (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Gather as fast as now | We gather at least as much salt per person per hour as with the dantala, so the tool does not cost us time | High | Agariya salt-making families (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Performance | Keep the floor and the crystals | The hand-hardened pan floor is not cut or cracked and the large crystals are not crushed | High | Agariya salt-making families (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-005 | Logistics | Carry it between pans | Two to four of us can carry every part between pans and along the bunds | High | Agariya salt-making families; producer groups (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-006 | Environment and durability | Last the season | It keeps working through a harvest in brine, salt, heat and dust if we rinse it each day | High | Agariya salt-making families; producer groups and NGOs (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-007 | Deployment and assembly | Quick to set up | We can set it up at the start of a working session without losing the cool hours | Medium | Agariya salt-making families (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-008 | Cost | Affordable to fund | A producer group or NGO can fund a set within its budget for a pilot, and the cost is known | High | Producer groups and NGOs (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-009 | Safety | No new injuries | Nobody is hurt by a loaded rope, a crank or gear, or an anchor that pulls out | High | Agariya salt-making families; women salt workers; producer groups (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-010 | Regulatory | Within our access conditions | The tool is hand powered, with no engine or motor, so it fits the conditions under which we may work in the Rann | High | Forest department; Agariya families (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-011 | Maintenance | Build and repair in town | A workshop in a town near the Rann can build it and repair it from common steel, plastic and bought parts | Medium | Local fabricators (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-012 | Performance | Reach across our pans | It reaches across the pans we work, which are tens of metres wide | Medium | Agariya salt-making families (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-013 | Regulatory | Leave nothing behind | It leaves no structure or lasting mark in the sanctuary when we move on | High | Forest department (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-014 | Usability | A family crew | A family crew of about four can work it without hiring extra hands | Medium | Agariya salt-making families (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-015 | Maintenance | Easy daily care | Daily care is a rinse and a little grease, and the parts that wear can be replaced on the spot | Medium | Agariya salt-making families; local fabricators (PRB Table 1) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
