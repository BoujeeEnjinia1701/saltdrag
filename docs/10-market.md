# SaltDrag market and viability

SaltDrag is for families who make salt by hand in small pans, first the Agariyas of the Little Rann of Kutch, who may not use machines and earn a small share of the price of their salt; nothing on the market gathers salt from the bund by hand, and the nearest bought parts (a hand winch, a rope puller) are built for lifting or occasional pulling, not for a season in brine. An open design lets producer groups and town workshops build, repair and adapt it without a supplier. Prices and benchmarks are cited with the date they were checked. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

Main cost lines of option A, from the bill of materials: fabrication labour and galvanising USD 450, side frames USD 220, drums USD 244, gears USD 190, rake blade USD 190, pull ropes USD 190. For comparison, 100 m of 12 mm double braid polyester rope (breaking strength about 2,957 kg) costs GBP 140 from a UK rope maker (RopeServices UK, checked 2026-10-25), in line with the USD 95 allowed for each 45 m rope. The lower-cost concept options are about USD 1,400 (option B) and USD 1,800 (option C) a set (SDG-OPT-001).

## Open route

SaltDrag spreads as a design, not a product. A producer group or NGO commissions a town workshop near the Rann (Gandhidham, Dhrangadhra or Kharaghoda) to build a set from the drawings, the making sketches and the build plan; gears, bearings, anchors and rope are bought from stockists. The partner trains crews and keeps spares. Everything is published under CERN-OHL-S-2.0, so any improvement made by a workshop or a group stays open, and the hand capstan is offered as a common block with SiltHaul so that one proven capstan serves both. No sale, margin or certification is planned at TRL 3: SaltDrag is published as an open engineering reference, not certified equipment.
